# PRODUCT.md — Megama d.o.o.

## Product Purpose

Corporate website for Megama d.o.o., a Slovenian engineering firm specializing in Non-Destructive Testing (NDT), welding supervision, quality assurance documentation, and technical consulting. The site serves as the company's primary digital presence — establishing credibility, showcasing expertise, and generating inbound project inquiries.

## Users

- **Primary**: Plant managers, quality engineers, and procurement officers at industrial facilities (power plants, refineries, shipyards, pipeline operators) evaluating NDT service providers.
- **Secondary**: Welding coordinators and QA/QC managers seeking certified inspection partners for specific projects.
- **Tertiary**: Potential employees and industry peers assessing the company's capabilities.

Users are technical professionals who value precision, certifications, and proven track records over flashy marketing. They access the site primarily on desktop during work hours, often in office environments with standard lighting.

brand

## Brand Personality

- **Precise**: Every measurement matters. The brand communicates with engineering exactness.
- **Trustworthy**: EN ISO 9712 certified, Level III qualified personnel. Trust is earned through credentials and results.
- **Modern yet grounded**: Advanced technology (ultrasonic, radiographic, magnetic testing) presented with sophisticated, confident design — not sterile or clinical.
- **Slovenian roots, international standards**: Bilingual (SL/EN), operating under EU norms.

## Tone

Confident, technical, understated. Never salesy or hyperbolic. Let the work speak. Numbers and certifications matter more than adjectives.

## Anti-References

- Generic corporate SaaS templates with purple gradients and identical card grids
- Stock-photo-heavy industrial sites with hard hats and handshakes
- Overly minimal "tech startup" aesthetics that feel hollow for an engineering firm
- Cluttered specification-dump pages with no visual hierarchy

## Strategic Principles

1. **Show the method, not just the name** — Each NDT method (UT, PT, MT, RT, etc.) should be visually demonstrated, not just listed.
2. **Certifications as trust signals** — EN ISO standards are first-class UI elements, not footnotes.
3. **Dark, precise, premium** — The design reflects the precision of the work itself.
4. **Bilingual parity** — Slovenian and English have equal treatment; neither is an afterthought.
